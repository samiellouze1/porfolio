Work Experience

Fidelity Information Services (FIS)

I currently work at Fidelity Information Services (FIS), where I contribute to the development and maintenance of backend systems within the capital markets domain. I joined FIS after completing my final academic project, leveraging both my academic foundation in capital markets and my strong proficiency in Java, Spring Boot, and the broader Java ecosystem.
I am part of the Margin team, which is responsible for computing Initial Margin Requirements and related financial metrics. These calculations are centralized within a core library and exposed through a backend application called Margin Engine, built using Java and OSGi. This engine serves multiple downstream systems, including back-office platforms like UBIX and risk monitoring tools such as Risk Advisor. The overall stack spans Java, Spring, OSGi, and Angular. During my onboarding, I contributed to maintenance and incremental improvements across Margin Engine and its frontend counterpart, Margin Advisor. I was then entrusted with maintaining a critical UBIX module responsible for orchestrating margin calculation requests across multiple markets, including FMX and OCC. This experience required me to quickly adapt to a complex legacy ecosystem involving C, Python, Java, and PL/SQL, significantly strengthening my ability to navigate and modernize heterogeneous systems. Since June 2025, I have been actively involved in the redevelopment of Risk Advisor, a risk monitoring platform designed to track margin metrics, financial balances, and generate actionable alerts. I played a key role in designing and implementing the database layer, writing optimized SQL-based CRUD operations aligned with a collaboratively defined domain model. To support frontend performance and usability, I engineered a robust data access layer enabling advanced querying capabilities, including dynamic filtering, sorting, pagination, and data expansion. In parallel, I developed a reusable integration library to interact with the platform’s REST APIs, facilitating the ingestion of referential data (financial instruments, products, futures, and calls) as well as transactional data such as trades, positions, and balances. I subsequently integrated this library into UBIX, ensuring seamless synchronization between systems. I also led the implementation of financial balances within Risk Advisor, incorporating them into the alerting framework and limit monitoring workflows. This included designing and computing derived indicators, combining margin data and financial balances through custom arithmetic operations to deliver enhanced risk insights. Through this experience, I have developed a strong appreciation for building high-performance, scalable systems in complex financial environments. I have worked across multiple applications of varying maturity, gaining a holistic understanding of enterprise system architecture. I thrive in environments that balance technical rigor with innovation, and I continuously challenge myself to go beyond established patterns—seeking not only to understand how systems work, but how they can be improved.
