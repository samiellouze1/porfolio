Courses, certifications, and skills beyond the day job

I earned the AWS Certified Developer – Associate (DVA-C02) certification to validate hands-on skills in building and operating secure, scalable applications on AWS. The exam covered core services such as compute, storage, networking, and identity, alongside deployment patterns and operational best practices. This credential reflects my commitment to cloud-native development and complements my backend engineering work with practical AWS fluency you can rely on in production environments.
As a hands-on follow-up to my AWS learning, I built a small library management style project that applied what I studied: IAM-aware access patterns, container-friendly deployment assumptions, and observable APIs. It was a practical sandbox to connect certification concepts to real code structure, error handling, and iterative delivery—similar to how I approach features at work, but scoped for experimentation and learning.
