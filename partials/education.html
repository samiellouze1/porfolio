Education

National School of Computer Science - ENSI

I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science from ENSI (Ecole Nationale des Sciences de l'Informatique), University of Manouba, Tunisia. I joined ENSI in 2020 after the national competitive entrance exam for engineering preparatory studies, where I placed 66th nationwide. The curriculum balanced rigorous theory with hands-on practice across the full breadth of computer science.
Core coursework covered web development, object-oriented programming, concurrency and complexity, software architecture and modeling, databases, and Linux--giving me a solid base to build on as a backend engineer. The program also emphasized mathematics: probability, statistics, and the foundations of machine learning, which sharpened how I reason about uncertainty and data-driven systems. In my final year I specialized in finance and capital markets. ENSI's broad CS foundation meant I could have gone deeper in almost any IT domain; I chose this track because I wanted to understand how exchanges work, how market participants manage risk and return, and how derivatives and instruments fit together. Coursework touched on value-at-risk (VaR), banking fundamentals, and how trading venues operate. That specialization aligned with my first role in the industry. Throughout the degree I worked on team projects under real deadlines--practice in decomposing messy problems, designing maintainable systems, and shipping code that others could extend. I also learned to communicate like an engineer: clear technical writing, structured design notes, and presentations that work for both peers and non-specialist stakeholders.
