Education

National School of Computer Science - ENSI

I earned my Computer Science Master's degree from ENSI (Ecole Nationale des Sciences de l'Informatique), University of Manouba. My academic track focused on software engineering foundations, systems thinking, and data-driven problem solving, with strong emphasis on practical implementation and project delivery.
This experience shaped the analytical discipline and technical rigor I apply today in backend engineering and real-world financial software environments. During my years at ENSI, I worked on team-based projects that strengthened my ability to design robust systems, break down complex problems, and deliver maintainable solutions under realistic constraints. The program also helped me build strong communication habits and engineering discipline, from documenting technical decisions to presenting project outcomes clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
